## Brand & Style

This design system channels the sun-baked, tactile expanse of high-desert terrain. It balances utilitarian productivity with an organic, grounded warmth. Tailored for creative thinkers, researchers, and project architects, the interface rejects the sterile coldness of blue-gray SaaS defaults in favor of mineral pigments, sun-bleached parchment tones, and baked clay accents.

The aesthetic philosophy draws from **warm tactile minimalism** combined with **editorial craftsmanship**. Visual elements feel physically seated into the sand plane rather than floating in generic space. Structural geometry remains crisp and disciplined, avoiding playful over-softness to maintain clarity during deep focus. Every screen conveys quiet presence, resilience, and unhurried clarity.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in arid landscape minerals:

- **Base Canvas (`#EED8B8`)**: A warm desert parchment tone providing an organic, low-glare ground for sustained viewing sessions.
- **Surface Elevation (`#F7EADB`, `#FFF6EC`)**: Sunlit cream and warm alabaster planes that gently rise above the base canvas for cards, flyouts, and modal dialogs.
- **Primary Accent (`#D9531E` / `#E05A1B`)**: Searing desert terracotta and vibrant coyote-orange, reserved for principal call-to-actions, energetic active states, and critical path focus.
- **Secondary Mineral Tone (`#8C6A48`)**: Desert rock and brushwood brown for secondary actions, muted badges, and foundational framing.
- **Tertiary Mineral (`#2B4C47`)**: Pinon pine and desert sage green used sparingly for positive confirmations, tag accents, and system success states.
- **Ink & Typography (`#231F1D`, `#3F3935`)**: Warm coyote charcoal and scorched slate delivering AAA contrast against the sandy backdrop without the harshness of pure black.
- **Divider & Border Rules (`#DAC09D`, `#CBB08C`)**: Low-contrast silt tones that delineate zones without fracturing the composition.

## Typography

Typography establishes an architectural, technical balance between the rugged landscape and modern software precision:

- **Display & Headlines (`Space Grotesk`)**: Provides geometric certainty with subtle idiosyncratic details that feel like structural field equipment. Used for page headers, card titles, KPI metrics, and section banners.
- **Body & Operational Labels (`Hanken Grotesk`)**: An ultra-clean grotesque with open apertures that ensures crisp legibility against textured sand tones across dense data views, tables, and long-form notes.
- **Numerical & Code Ratios**: When rendering coordinates, timestamps, or raw IDs, fall back to proportional tabular figures within `Hanken Grotesk` or an inline monospace token to reinforce field-log documentation.

## Layout & Spacing

The spatial engine utilizes a disciplined 12-column adaptive fluid grid punctuated by wide margins that honor the openness of desert horizons.

- **Desktop (1200px+)**: 12 columns with 2rem (32px) gutters and 3rem (48px) perimeter canvas margin. Layouts prioritize horizontal breathability, grouping contextual tools into unified sandstone-like panels.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px)**: 8 columns with 1.5rem (24px) gutters and 2rem (32px) canvas margins. Multi-column tools stack into tiered workspaces.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with 1.25rem (20px) gutters and 1.25rem margins. Primary workspaces collapse into full-width cards with explicit top-to-bottom procedural flow.
- **Vertical Spacing Cadence**: Governed strictly through multiples of `0.25rem` (4px). Structural sections separate at `space-xl` (36px), whereas atomic component contents lock together at `space-sm` (8px) and `space-md` (16px).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects generic artificial drop shadows in favor of **tonal layering and earth-toned ambient occlusion**:

- **Ground Tier (Canvas)**: `#EED8B8` serves as the bedrock surface. It represents non-interactive space and the overall frame.
- **Tier 1 (Surface Cards & Workspace Tiles)**: Rendered in `#F7EADB` (sun-warmed sand). Edges are defined by a crisp 1px structural stroke in `#DAC09D`. Instead of black shadows, use a warm ambient drop: `0px 2px 8px rgba(78, 55, 33, 0.06), 0px 1px 2px rgba(78, 55, 33, 0.04)`.
- **Tier 2 (Popovers, Overlays & Elevated Cards)**: Rendered in `#FFF6EC` (high-noon parchment). Framed with a 1px border of `#CBB08C` and cast with a deeper terracotta-tinted shade: `0px 8px 24px rgba(78, 55, 33, 0.10), 0px 2px 6px rgba(78, 55, 33, 0.05)`.
- **Tier 3 (Active Draggable Nodes & Modals)**: Lifted cleanly from the plane with `0px 16px 36px rgba(58, 38, 22, 0.14)` and a sharp 1.5px accent hairline on hover states.

## Shapes

The system implements a calibrated, semi-tactile corner radius (`level 1` - Soft). 

- **Components (Buttons, Inputs, Badges)**: 0.25rem (4px) corner radius. This gives actionable controls a carved, chiseled masonry character rather than bubbly consumer-app curves.
- **Containers & Workspace Cards**: 0.5rem (8px) corner radius (`rounded-lg`), grounding large data panels with architectural discipline.
- **Floating Modals & Canvas Trays**: 0.75rem (12px) corner radius (`rounded-xl`), creating soft delineation when layering dynamic instruments over stationary boards.
- **Pill Exceptions**: Micro status indicators and tag badges may employ full pill curves only when displaying non-interactive scalar metrics.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid terracotta `#D9531E` fill with `#FFF6EC` text. 0.25rem corner radius, 10px 18px padding for medium size. Hover transitions to baked blaze `#E05A1B` with a subtle `0px 2px 6px rgba(217, 83, 30, 0.3)` aura. Active state presses down by 1px.
- **Secondary**: `#F7EADB` background, 1px border in `#DAC09D`, text in charcoal `#231F1D`. On hover, the surface shifts to `#FFF6EC` with border deepening to `#CBB08C`.
- **Ghost / Text**: Transparent ground, text in `#3F3935`. Hover introduces a 15% `#E7CEAD` tinted surface wash.

### Cards & Panels
- Constructed from `#F7EADB` with a uniform 1px `#DAC09D` boundary.
- Internal headers use a subtle bottom divider line (`1px solid #E6CDAE`).
- Workspace cards feature an intentional 16px to 24px inner padding, ensuring typography never touches boundary lines.

### Text Inputs & Form Controls
- **Input Ground**: `#FFFBF6` to create an inviting, high-clarity writing surface nestled within the `#EED8B8` sheet.
- **Default State**: 1px border in `#DAC09D`, placeholder text in `#7E7166`, text color in `#231F1D`.
- **Focus State**: Hairline border switches to vibrant coyote terracotta `#D9531E` accompanied by a 2px outer glow in `rgba(217, 83, 30, 0.18)`. Background remains pristine.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square 18px boxes (0.125rem radius) and 18px circular radios with a 1.5px `#BFA482` perimeter over `#FFFBF6`.
- Checked state transitions immediately to `#D9531E` with an ivory `#FFF6EC` checkmark glyph.

### Chips & Metadata Tags
- Height: 24px. Padding: 2px 8px. Typography: `label-sm` uppercase tracking.
- Neutral variant: `#EAD1B0` background with `#4D4036` text.
- Terracotta variant: `#FCEEE8` ground with `#A83A0F` text and an optional `#D9531E` leading accent dot.

### Lists & Activity Feeds
- Row items possess no heavy separating lines; separation is created by alternating micro-spacing or low-contrast hairline borders (`rgba(140, 106, 72, 0.15)`).
- Hovering an interactive list record activates an elevation wash of `#FFF6EC` with 4px corner rounding.

### Workspace Canvas Elements
- **Node Blocks**: Cream slabs `#FFF6EC` with a left structural border (3px) in `#D9531E` to distinguish active task nodes from passive reference modules.
- **Canvas Viewport Grid**: Discrete 24px crosshairs or stippled dots colored `#DEC4A3` set across the native `#EED8B8` terrain.
